## Digital Business Consultant

At [PETA](https://wwww.august.style/awards-viral-social), I owned the data, set the process, reviewed the cuts; I owned the process end to end until I shifted towards scaling, optimizing, and future-proofing the visual and social first focus we established across the, now well-oiled, massive global machine. I valued the autonomy and agency I had worked hard for very much, and it wasn't until my mentor, whom I built everything with, left to start her own consultancy. Soon after she left I was talking with [Chelsea Williams](https://www.linkedin.com/in/naftelberg/), a long time friend with whom I always ended up talking shop with. We shared strategies, advice, and pushed each other to grow. So when she told me she was doing the same as Helena, it started to seem like a viable option. 

### Selling Outcomes Not Hours

Having run every part of the digital-marketing spectrum, it seems logical to take on a similar challenge again, but for a SMO who need a consultant who could own every process end to end, building out her entire digital business.

#### Hawaii Physicians Urgent Need for COVID-19 PPE

Before digging into things with my first client, I was hit with some urgent work coming up with a solution to help coordinate the very limited supply of PPE in Hawaii for Physicians during the COVID-19 pandemic.

    - Squarespace was my best bet for speed and ease of maintenance thanks to experience 
    - Setup up fresh email account for all account creation on the project 
    - Added Mailchimp as I figured they could send health network updates in real time

    - Structured what seemed like would be a logical hierarchy of information 
    - Upon finalizing things I gave the assistant a heads up that I was just about complete 
    - I made things a bit briefer everywhere and the action steps more prominent 
    - Ensured non-technical staff could publish real-time with no developer needed
    - She was very happy with the final product 
    - It was shared widely, ahead of any government efforts in the state
