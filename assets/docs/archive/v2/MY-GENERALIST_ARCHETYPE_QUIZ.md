## The "Four C's Framework" *Need to complete and identify* 

  * **TRUTH! "Reminder: Get your foot in the door and then you can up-sell yourself & create new opportunities" because this has literally always been the path I've taken in any employment** 

### Narrow-in, Without Niching Down 

1. Core Skill
   - What are you down-right brilliant at?
   - What gives you the most energy?
   - What do people always ask you for help with?

2. Complementary Skill
   - What's an unexpected or novel compliment to your core?
   - What skills do you combine that few others can say they do?
   - eg: Creative writing (core) + Data analysis (complementary)

3. Compassion Skill
   - What's your top "human" skill?
   - What are the things you do that Al can not replace?
   - eg: highly empathetic, critical thinker, ability to motivate

4. Context Skill
   - In which contexts do you work best?
   - When are you most "lit up" at work?
   - eg: the intersection of entrepreneurship & the arts
