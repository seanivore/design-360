# The Generalist 360º Playbook

## Pyramid Traits
- Broad expertise: print, digital, video, OOH, AI systems. Not dabbling—depth in each.
- Generalist mindset: pattern spotter, chaos tamer, system builder. Connects dots others miss.
- Emotional intelligence: reads rooms, clients, markets. Knows when to push, pivot, or shut up.

## Atomic Influence
- Reputation > audience. Trusted, everywhere that counts.
- Long game: builds systems, not just assets. Work keeps delivering after you’re gone.
- Helpfulness: ships outcomes, teaches, documents, makes it repeatable.

## Adaptive Economy
- Skills expire fast. Learn, unlearn, relearn—on repeat.
- Leverage (tools, network, distribution), learning (commercial competence), spikiness (go deep, then apply).
- Portfolio career: operate like a company—product, ops, marketing, people, culture, finance.

## Vibe Work
- Humans = heart, AI = hustle. Taste, timing, story from you. Scale, speed, options from AI.
- Outcome-driven: $0.003 CPE, 100M+ views, 10x video scale, city-scale OOH, agentic automation.

## II Ladder
- Horizontal growth: designer who codes, marketer who writes, strategist who builds.
- Income portfolio: consulting, digital assets, equity, content, fractional roles.
- Ownership & value: assets that live beyond a gig—templates, systems, playbooks.

## Generalists in Big Orgs
- Ops, program, product, cross-functional: dot-connector, system-maker.
- Mastery is synthesis, not a single lane.

## 3 Core Traits (Proof)
1. Emotional intelligence: led advocacy, 100M+ views, Shorty finalist, OOH taglines, art direction that lands.
2. Creative adaptability: print, digital, video, motion, OOH. Jumps formats, keeps story sharp.
