# Alternative Job Hunting 
*I am not difficult. I am specific. I've built a workflow that only rewards that. The right rooms will recognize it immediately.*

## Overview  

  * **Engagement models that dodge corporate nonsense** 
  - 6-week paid pilot. One KPI. Single exec sponsor
  - Retainer with a fixed ritual: weekly ship, one metric, one decision meeting
  - Bounty menu. Price per artifact. Pattern doc, prototype, playbook, handoff

### Approach 

  * **How to present yourself without sanding off edges**

  - **One-liner:** I take half-ideas and constraints, build the system, and move one number fast.
  - **Proof:** 3 case cards. Context, pattern spotted, move made, metric moved. One page each.
      - Portfolio revamp with new text taking on this structure 
      - Find best past examples, plan proposals 
  - **Offer:** Here is a scoped pilot and the price. Start Monday.

  * **TL;DR**
  - Cold DM with a specific, low-ask question.
  - Save your full energy for the right rooms—don’t dim your light, just aim it.
  - Find the nerds, the fixators, the people who get it.
  - You’re not too much—you’re just not in the right room yet.

### Taking the Shot 

  * **Interview script that keeps you in control**

  - **Open with outcome:** What 90-day result makes this hire a win for you?
  - **Mirror constraints out loud:** I am hearing speed, compliance, zero headcount. Anything missing?
  - **Show your brain:** sketch boxes and flows while talking. Make it visible and simple.
  - **Close with a deal:** Here is a 6-week pilot plan, price, deliverables, and risks.

  * **Boundaries that protect your energy**
  - No unpaid spec. No vague "we'll figure it out."
  - One decision maker. One KPI. Weekly cadence. Or you walk.
  - If they flinch at metrics or pilots, it is not your house.

  * **Where to point your shots**
  - Health tech and climate tools that need clear flows and trust
  - AI platforms and dev tools that die without good onboarding and demos
  - Fintech infra that lives or dies by friction reduction

### Pitch Specifics 

  * **Fast lead gen that actually works**
  - Ship a public teardown a week. Pattern, fix, expected metric lift. Keep it short and visual.
  - DM founders with a one-slide pilot. Specific to their product, not generic advice.
  - Ask for warm intros from engineers and PMs, not recruiters.

  * **Pricing that respects the value**
  - Pilot: 15–40k for 4–6 weeks depending on scope and access
  - Retainer: 6–12k per month for one metric, one ritual, one sponsor
  - Success kicker: When the KPI lifts above target

## Overseas Job Hunting Notes, Using Above Tactic 

### Cold-applying overseas rarely works. Switch to a targeted, referral-led sprint with a pilot offer.

  - Visa friction: Many roles silently filter “requires work authorization/local language.”
  - No local referral: EU teams hire via warm intros, not boards.
  - Role mismatch: Your hybrid skill set doesn’t map to rigid titles.
  - Portfolio not localized: No 60s demos, no “EU-friendly” CV, no work-authorization note.

### A 2-week overseas sprint (tight, repeatable)

Week 1: Build the “Export Kit”
 • One-page EU CV: concise, skills-first, no US-style fluff. Add a Work Authorization line: “Open to D7/Pay Limit/Employer-of-Record; relocation-ready.”
 • 3 case cards: Context → Pattern → Move → Outcome (1 page each).
 • 2 demo reels (45–60s): screen-capture you walking a prototype; subtitles on.
 • Pilot menu: 6-week scoped offer with price range and KPI options.
 • Geo landing line: “Available CET-friendly hours. Past work with X/Y sectors.”

Week 1–2: Target and outreach
 • Build a list of 30 teams (10 per geo) where your work matters:
 ▫ Denmark: health/infra/climate + AI tooling. Where to find: The Hub, Jobindex, WorkInDenmark, LinkedIn “Design Manager” in Copenhagen.
 ▫ Portugal: Landing.jobs, Indiecareers in Lisbon/Porto, EU-Startups, Webflow/Figma/AI startups.
 ▫ Remote-first EU: Wellfound (EU filter), DJN, Otta (location-flex), company sites.
 • Find 2 people per company: design lead + founding PM/CTO.

Outreach that gets replies (sequence)
 • Day 0 DM/email
Subject: Quick pattern for [Product] onboarding
Hi [Name] — I work at the intersection of AI UX and systems. I mapped a 2-step pattern that could cut [your X friction] by ~20–30%. Here’s a 45s demo. If it resonates, I can run a 6-week pilot: one KPI, weekly ship, minimal lift. Want the one-pager?
 • Day 3 follow-up
Pinging this once. Happy to send the 1-pager or leave you with the checklist I use to find onboarding drop-offs in AI tools.
 • Day 7 final
Closing the loop. If priorities shift, here’s my pilot menu and calendar link for a 15-min scoping chat.

Referral ask (don’t open with “refer me”)
 • “Sanity check ask: Does my 6-week pilot map to your current priorities? If yes, who’s the best owner to show a 45s demo? If no, is there one team who’d actually use this?”

Where to fish (specific channels)
 • Denmark: The Hub, Jobindex, WorkInDenmark, TechBBQ Slack, CPHUX community.
 • Portugal: Landing.jobs, Startup Portugal, Factory Lisbon/Porto communities, Beta-i.
 • Remote EU: EU-Startups Job Board, Y Combinator’s Work at a Startup (filter EU-friendly), Indie Hackers, AI/UX Discords.
 • Health/climate/fintech Slacks: look for “jobs-and-collab” channels.

Positioning that removes the visa objection
 • Offer remote-first with CET overlap + Employer-of-Record via Deel/Remote as a bridge.
 • Note clean visa paths when relevant:
 ▫ Denmark: Pay Limit Scheme (high-salary fast-track), Positive List (shortage roles).
 ▫ Portugal: Digital Nomad/D7 (income-based), easy residency runway.
 ▫ Uruguay/Chile: straightforward residency; contract via EoR.

Your lane (titles that fit you)
 • Creative Technologist, Prototyping Lead (AI + UX)
 • Product Designer, Growth Systems
 • AI UX/Agent Workflows Designer
 • Narrative/Demo Architect (dev tools)

Protect your energy (timeboxes and filters)
 • 30-minute cap to tailor an app. If it takes more, it’s not a “hell yes.”
 • Only pursue roles you’d accept tomorrow if offered.
 • Weekly goals: 10 targeted messages, 2 demos, 1 live scoping call, 1 shipped teardown.

Portfolio quick wins (same day)
 • Add a “Pilot” page with 3 scope examples, prices, and KPIs.
 • Localize a version of your CV for EU (shorter, results forward).
 • Put the 45–60s demo links top-right on your site. Subtitles on. No music.
 • Add this line: “Available remote EU; relocation open. LGBTQ-friendly teams preferred.”

Paste-ready assets

The shift is simple: stop asking to be chosen; offer a small, valuable win. Overseas teams respond to momentum, not perfect resumes. Run the sprint, ship two demos, and book the calls. You’ll feel the difference in a week.
