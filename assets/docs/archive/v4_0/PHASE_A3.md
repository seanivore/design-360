## Art Director for a Privacy Protocol

I spent the Silent Labs years as Art Director for a Web3 privacy protocol — and the whole job came
down to one problem. Privacy technology is abstract. Cryptography, decentralized identity, data
sovereignty: real, important, and impossible to feel. My work was to make it human.

That meant two audiences at once. Developers who would build on the protocol and validate every
claim for accuracy, and consumers who would benefit from it but had no patience for a whitepaper.
Everything below is a different attempt to serve both without losing either.

### **Building the Brand System**

The foundation was a complete brand identity and creative direction — the visual language the rest
of the work would extend.

IMAGE: `flow-web3-privacy-art-direction-1.webp`
NEW MEDIA: brand-system board — logo, palette, type, and a few collateral pieces (landing page,
social template, pitch deck spread) shown together.

  + Web3 Brand Creative Direction & Strategy
    - A full identity system that signaled innovation without the corporate blandness of fintech
      or the chaotic aesthetics of early crypto
    - Visual metaphors for privacy and security that avoided every lock-and-key cliché
    - Art direction carried across digital, social, and investor-facing collateral, each adapted to
      its audience while staying coherent

### **Making the Technology Legible**

Brand is only half of it. The other half was content — taking the protocol's most technical ideas
and redrawing them so they could be understood.

IMAGE: `flow-web3-privacy-art-direction-2.webp`
NEW MEDIA: a before/after of one DeFi concept — the dense technical version beside the progressive-
disclosure visual redesign.

  + Technical DeFi Content Simplification
    - Broke complex DeFi mechanics into visual hierarchies — understand it at a glance, drill in for
      depth
    - Used familiar chart idioms to represent novel financial structures
    - Satisfied developers who wanted accuracy while giving everyone else genuine clarity
  + Web3 Strategy Branding Productions
    - Motion design and hand illustration used specifically to break down sophisticated concepts
    - A reusable visual framework for communicating each new protocol feature and update

The breakthrough wasn't a diagram. It was a character. If privacy is a civil right, then it needs a
story and someone to root for — so I drew one.
