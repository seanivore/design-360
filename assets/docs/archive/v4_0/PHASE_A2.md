## Going Out On My Own

When I left in-house viral marketing, I didn't specialize — I did the opposite. I started a
one-person digital consultancy where a client could hand me a brand and get back the whole stack:
strategy, copy, a design system, and a website they could actually run themselves.

That generalist instinct is the through-line of everything below. None of this work used AI. It was
hand-drawn art, hand-written copy, and platforms — Squarespace, Kajabi, Webflow — chosen for what
the client could maintain after I handed it over.

### **Emergency Work on a 24-Hour Clock**

My first proof that "generalist" meant "fast" came from the Hawaii COVID-19 Taskforce. They needed a
public health communications platform live immediately — not in a sprint, in a day.

  + Public Health Response Platform — Hawaii COVID-19 Taskforce
    - Built on Squarespace with a 24-hour turnaround during an active emergency

    - Architected so non-technical taskforce staff could publish real-time updates with no

    - The lesson held: an effective emergency platform comes from clarity, not technical complexity

## Brand-First Client Work

Most of my consulting clients didn't need a website so much as they needed an identity — and then a
website to carry it. The wellness space made that especially clear, because in an industry built on
trust, a stock template reads as a stranger.

### **A Wellness Brand Drawn Entirely By Hand**

My longest-running client was a body-awareness coach and online personal trainer. I built her
digital presence twice, and across both builds I drew every brand element by hand.

*her*. That's a capability, not a nostalgia: knowing when the human touch is the deliverable.

## The Bridge Into Code

After years of no-code, I wanted back into real HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and I wanted to do it
the way I'd do everything after: with AI as a pair, but on a system strict enough to keep AI honest.

### **My First Hand-Built Site Since Going No-Code**

The Modular Portfolio Build was that first site. It looks like a portfolio; it's really a template
system designed to prevent AI coding chaos.

IMAGE: `flow-self-employed-web-consultancy-5.webp`
SOURCE: `assets/.media/vid-modular-portfolio-build.mp4`; YouTube `https://youtu.be/OLZHwv2o-zQ`.

  + Modular Portfolio System
    - A comprehensive `CONVENTIONS.md` that doubles as human reference and AI training material
    - Reusable component patterns — navigation, project cards, content sections — consistent across
      50+ entries
    - Tag-based filtering in vanilla JavaScript, kept lightweight on purpose
    - Repeatable enough that AI could generate new pages matching the existing structure across many
      separate sessions without breaking patterns

This is the hinge of the whole portfolio: the last project of the foundation years and the first
one built for the agentic workflow that defines everything in Phase B.
